Military Skills Translation Notebook

Capture what you did—not just what your title was.

This local notebook helps organize civilian-language evidence before opening an official military occupation translator, Career Portal, resume template, or counselor session.

A better translation question

What responsibility did you actually carry?

A military title rarely tells the whole story. Think in terms of people led, systems operated, logistics coordinated, safety standards followed, decisions made under pressure, training delivered, documentation maintained, equipment managed, quality controlled, or problems solved.

Do not enter sensitive information here. Never store classified information, controlled operational details, Social Security numbers, claim numbers, medical information, passwords, security answers, protected personal data, or anything you would not place in an ordinary civilian resume draft. This notebook is for general career language only.
