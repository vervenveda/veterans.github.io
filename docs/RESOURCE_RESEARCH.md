# RESOURCE_RESEARCH.md

Research snapshot: August 2026.

## Housing and homelessness
Current VA homelessness pathways reviewed:
- VA Homeless Help
- National Call Center for Homeless Veterans
- HUD-VASH
- Supportive Services for Veteran Families (SSVF)
- Veterans Justice Programs

The page does not promise bed, voucher, grant, or provider availability. Users are directed to authoritative VA pathways and told to verify locally.
