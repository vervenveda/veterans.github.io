# RESOURCE_RESEARCH.md

Research review: **August 12, 2026**

This document supports the Veterans Career & Resource Hub modernization. Official veteran employment, education, and entrepreneurship links were checked against current federal sources before inclusion.

## Career and employment

### U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Careers & Employment
- Official VA careers/employment gateway.
- Veteran Readiness & Employment (VR&E / Chapter 31) remains the primary VA vocational rehabilitation and employment pathway for eligible Veterans and service members with service-connected disabilities.
- VA currently describes five VR&E support-and-services tracks:
  1. Reemployment
  2. Rapid Access to Employment
  3. Self-Employment
  4. Employment Through Long-Term Services
  5. Independent Living
- Official URLs:
  - https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/
  - https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/
  - https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/programs/

### U.S. Department of Labor — Veterans' Employment and Training Service (VETS)
- DOL VETS provides veteran employment resources, state-specific resources, transition information, American Job Center connections, and other employment services.
- Official URLs:
  - https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/
  - https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/veterans/veterans-employment-services
  - https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/programs/findajob

### My Next Move for Veterans
- Developed and maintained through the O*NET system under U.S. Department of Labor sponsorship.
- Supports keyword search, industry exploration, interest profiling, and military occupation-to-civilian career transition search.
- Current site describes more than 900 civilian careers.
- Official URL:
  - https://www.mynextmove.org/vets/

### Federal employment
- USAJOBS maintains a veteran hiring-path information page describing Veterans' Preference and veteran-specific hiring options.
- Official URLs:
  - https://help.usajobs.gov/working-in-government/unique-hiring-paths/veterans
  - https://www.usajobs.gov/

### Registered Apprenticeship
- Apprenticeship.gov provides specific service-member and veteran information, including military experience, advanced standing, SkillBridge relationships, and GI Bill-related apprenticeship information.
- Official URLs:
  - https://www.apprenticeship.gov/help/what-apprenticeship-programs-exist-service-members-andor-veterans
  - https://www.apprenticeship.gov/

## Education and training

### VA Education & Training
- VA Education & Training / GI Bill resources remain the authoritative source for benefit rules.
- The GI Bill Comparison Tool supports comparison of schools, employers, and approved programs.
- Official URLs:
  - https://benefits.va.gov/gibill/
  - https://www.va.gov/education/
  - https://www.va.gov/education/gi-bill-comparison-tool/schools-and-employers

### Khaemenes educational resources
- Khaemenes Career Portal and Higher Learning are internal educational/exploration resources.
- They **do not imply VA approval, GI Bill approval, institutional accreditation, transfer credit, or benefit eligibility**.
- URLs:
  - https://vervenveda.com/Khaemenes_Higher_Learning.github.io/Career/
  - https://vervenveda.com/Khaemenes_Higher_Learning.github.io/

## Veteran entrepreneurship

### SBA Office of Veterans Business Development
- SBA's Office of Veterans Business Development supports veterans, service members, National Guard and Reserve members, military spouses, and family members with business-development programs and services.
- Official URL:
  - https://www.sba.gov/about-sba/sba-locations/headquarters-offices/office-veterans-business-development

### Veterans Business Outreach Centers (VBOC)
- VBOCs provide workshops, training, counseling, mentorship, and referrals.
- Official URL:
  - https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance/resource-partners/veterans-business-outreach-center-vboc-program

### Boots to Business
- SBA entrepreneurship education offered as part of the broader military transition ecosystem.
- Official URL:
  - https://www.sba.gov/sba-learning-platform/boots-business

### Veteran contracting
- SBA veteran contracting assistance includes Veteran Small Business Certification / VetCert information and veteran-owned federal contracting pathways.
- Official URL:
  - https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/veteran-contracting-assistance-programs

## Crisis support

The page uses the U.S. **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** phone route only. No additional crisis phone/SMS details are hard-coded into this build.

## Freshness and safety

Veteran benefit eligibility, employment services, hiring rules, education approval, contracting programs, local service availability, and office hours can change.

Therefore:
- Official-resource records in `mentor-manifest.json` are marked `requiresFreshnessCheck`.
- The page does not promise eligibility, approval, employment, grants, contracts, school benefits, housing placement, or service availability.
- Career notebook fields explicitly prohibit classified, operationally sensitive, medical, identity, claims, or credential-secret information.
